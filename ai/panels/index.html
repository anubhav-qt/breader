Drag a dot to move that corner: let go and it snaps to the border, unless ⌘ is held (Ctrl+Z: where you let go). Arrows nudge it a pixel (Shift: ten), Tab picks the next corner. Drag on bare page to draw a panel (Shift: anywhere), or double-click a panel to box it out to the gutter. X removes a panel (⌘X: all), [ and ] change its place in the order. B swaps in what Breader found. Ctrl+Z undoes, S saves, A and D go to the last and next page.
